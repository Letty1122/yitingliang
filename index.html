02 Individual research · 2026–present

TEMPO: Certified Progress Control for Multi-Humanoid Transport

A high-level shared-progress supervisor that coordinates paired humanoids carrying a shared payload, instead of modifying the low-level robot tracker. It preserves the demonstrated relative phase between robots while avoiding coordination failures caused by rigid fixed-clock playback.
